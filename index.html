Intro

Hello — I'm Raghuttam Hombal.

I'm a PhD student at the University of Manchester, in the Jodrell Bank Centre for Astrophysics. I work in PSS, the Pulsar and Transient Search Subsystem for the SKA Observatory. Alongside building the real-time software pipeline, my PhD focuses on RFI mitigation techniques, red noise removal and its after-effects, supervised by Prof. Ben Stappers and Dr. Lina Levin.
